Flood level sources: what the NWS and NOAA tags mean
NWS NWS forecast office flood levels
Set locally by the National Weather Service office for that stretch of coast: NWS Portland from Astoria to Florence, NWS Medford from Reedsport south. Each gauge names its office. These are the levels the office uses when it issues Coastal Flood Advisories and Warnings, and the flood stages on NWS river hydrographs. Rivers have NWS levels only.
NOAA NOAA derived flood levels
Calculated by NOAA's National Ocean Service with one nationwide formula from each tide gauge's tide range (NOAA Technical Report NOS CO-OPS 086, 2018). Used in NOAA's high-tide flooding reports and outlooks. No forecast office sets them, and on the Oregon coast they are usually lower than the NWS levels, so they turn orange sooner.
sources differ
The two sources put the same Reading at different levels. The color shown is the higher of the two, so neither agency's level is hidden, and the chip names the source it came from. Before acting, confirm with the forecast office named on that gauge which level it is working from.
"none published for this gauge"
NOAA's station records hold no levels from that source. For NWS, ask the named office whether it uses a local level.
