# Version 1 covers compound flooding only; tsunami comes from official warnings

Coastal Watch's first version shows only the inputs to compound flooding: tide gauges, offshore and nearshore wave buoys, and coastal river gages. DART tsunami buoys, ShakeAlert seismic stations, the OOI Endurance Array, HF radar and beach profile surveys are deliberately left out. Tsunami warnings for Oregon are issued by the National Tsunami Warning Center, and decision makers should act on those official messages, not on our own reading of raw DART pressure data; the status of the DART systems off Oregon is also unconfirmed. The other networks do not answer "is the coast flooding now or at the next high tide?". When tsunami is added, it will display or link official warning-center messages.

## Update, 25 November 2026

This scope carries over unchanged to the any-state version (ADR 0003): only the geography widens, not the hazards. Tsunami information for any coast comes from the official warning centers (NTWC, or PTWC for Hawaii and the Pacific territories), never from raw DART data.
